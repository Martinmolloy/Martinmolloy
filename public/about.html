Who we are

Built to put Irish roofs to work

Irish businesses pay some of the highest electricity prices in Europe, while the roofs over their heads do nothing. The physics is settled: a commercial roof in Ireland returns about 950 kWh for every kilowatt of panels on it, every year, for twenty-five years. Aura Energy exists to change that.

We design, install and look after the whole system, and you choose how to pay for it. On a Solar PPA, Aura funds and owns the system and you buy the power it makes at a fixed rate below your grid rate, locked for the term: no capital, no debt, no maintenance bills. Prefer to own it? We'll price the system outright, with any grants and tax reliefs you qualify for, and show you both options side by side.

Every system uses Tier-1 panels and inverters and is fitted by vetted Irish crews and certified electricians, after a structural check of the roof. On a PPA we build from our own balance sheet, so if an array underperforms, the shortfall is ours, not a line on your bill.

Business roofs are our focus, from farms and offices to warehouses and factories, and homeowners get the same standards through Aura Home Energy.
