Who we are

Built because the numbers work and the capital doesn't

Irish businesses pay some of the highest electricity prices in Europe, while the roofs over their heads do nothing. The physics is settled: a commercial roof in Ireland returns about 950 kWh for every kilowatt of panels on it, every year, for twenty-five years. What stops most businesses isn't doubt, it's the upfront cost.

Aura Energy exists to remove that barrier. We fund, design, install, own and operate the solar system on your roof, and you simply buy the power it makes at a fixed rate below your grid rate, locked for the term. No capital, no debt, no maintenance bills. Prefer to own the system outright? We design and build that too, and we'll show you both options side by side.

We're deliberately not a fund with a logo. Aura builds from its own balance sheet, uses tier-1 panels and inverters, and delivers through vetted Irish installation crews and certified electricians, with structural engineers checking every roof before it carries a system. If an array underperforms, the shortfall is ours, not a line on your bill. That's the whole model: we only do well when your roof does.

How we work

Four rules we don't bend

Skin in the game

We put our own money on your roof and get paid only for power it actually produces. Incentives aligned, in writing.

Straight numbers

Estimates come with their assumptions attached, savings are counted on self-consumed units only, and a free survey replaces every figure before you sign anything.

Built properly

Tier-1 equipment, Irish crews, structural checks before a single panel goes up, and no roof penetrations where clamping does the job.

There for the term

A PPA is a 20-year relationship. Monitoring, maintenance and repairs stay our problem, and the system is yours at the end.
