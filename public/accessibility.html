Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated: 30 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use auraenergy.ie, including people who rely on a keyboard, a screen reader or reduced motion. Our aim is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have built in

  • The whole site works with a keyboard, including the chat assistant, the forms and the estimator sliders, with a visible focus indicator throughout.
  • Text and buttons meet WCAG contrast minimums, including dark text on our orange buttons.
  • Images have text alternatives, form fields have labels, and form errors are announced and explained in plain words.
  • The site respects your reduced-motion setting: decorative animations are switched off when your system asks for that.
  • Text can be resized and the layout works on any screen size. Nothing flashes.

Known limitations

  • The rotating 3D roof sketch in the estimator and the sun scene in the solar panel section are visual aids. The same information is always available as text beside them.
  • Voice input in the chat is optional and depends on your browser. Typing always works.

Tell us what is not working

If any part of this site is hard to use with your setup, email info@auraenergy.ie and tell us what happened and what browser or assistive technology you were using. We aim to reply within five business days and to fix genuine barriers quickly.

Review

We test the site against WCAG 2.2 AA with automated checks and keyboard testing, and we review this statement when the site changes materially.
