Why Aura

A commercial roof in Ireland returns about 950 kWh for every kilowatt on it, every year, for twenty-five years.

Aura Energy exists because that number is reliable and the capital to unlock it usually isn't. So we put up the capital instead, take the performance risk, and sell you the output.

The PPA advantage

Pay less for every unit, from day one

Irish commercial electricity prices are among the highest in Europe, and they move with global markets. A Solar PPA locks a lower rate for the power your roof produces, giving you an immediate saving and long-term certainty.

Immediate savings

Your PPA rate is set below your current grid rate, so the saving starts the day the system is switched on, not after a payback period.

Price certainty

While grid prices fluctuate with gas markets, your solar rate is fixed and predictable, a genuine hedge for your energy budget.

Verified green energy

Every kWh is generated on your own roof: real, traceable decarbonisation for your ESG reporting and your customers.

Grants & incentives

The State pays for part of the roof

Irish commercial solar carries real public funding. Which scheme applies depends on what you are and how big the system is, and only one grant can go on any one system. As an SEAI registered installer, we handle the applications end to end.

Enhanced rates now open. SEAI has roughly doubled its business solar grant for new applications made between 6 October 2026 and 6 July 2027.

Up to €325,200

SEAI Non-Domestic Microgen

The main business scheme. Paid per kWp on a sliding scale, so the rate per kWp falls as the system gets bigger, for systems up to 1,000 kWp.

  • Businesses, farms, schools, community centres and non-profits
  • €600/kWp from 7 to 20 kWp, €400/kWp to 200 kWp, €300/kWp to 1,000 kWp
  • SEAI-registered installer, with approval in place before materials are bought or work starts
60% grant

TAMS 3 Solar Capital Investment

The farm route, run by the Department of Agriculture rather than SEAI. Pays 60% of approved costs up to a €90,000 investment ceiling per holding, so up to €54,000 in grant.

  • Systems up to 62 kWp, with batteries up to half the panel capacity
  • Sized to the farm's own yearly use, which can include one farm dwelling
  • Can't be combined with the SEAI grant on the same system
100% in year one

Accelerated Capital Allowance

A tax measure rather than a grant. Write the full cost off against trading profits in the year the system starts working, instead of over eight years. Runs to 31 December 2030.

  • Companies, sole traders and other businesses paying Irish tax
  • The panel model must be on SEAI's Triple E register
  • Claimed on your cost after any grant

Common questions

Quick answers before you switch

The questions Irish businesses ask us most. Don't see yours? Ask the team directly.

What is a Solar PPA?
We fund, install, own and maintain a solar system on your roof. You buy the electricity it produces at a fixed rate per kWh, set below your grid rate, so you save from day one with nothing to pay upfront.
How much will we save?
Typically up to 50% on every unit the system supplies, for example 16c against a 32c day rate. We confirm your exact rate after a free site assessment, side by side with what you pay now.
Who looks after the system, and what if it underperforms?
We do. Under the PPA we own, insure, monitor and maintain the system for the full term, and you only pay for the electricity it actually produces.
What happens at the end of the term, or if we move?
At the end of the term the system transfers to your business and its power is free from then on. If you sell or move, the agreement can pass to the new occupier, or you can buy the system out.
Should we choose the PPA or buy the system?
Choose the PPA to save without spending capital or carrying maintenance risk. Buy outright if you have the capital and want the biggest lifetime return, helped by SEAI grants and the Accelerated Capital Allowance. We quote both side by side.
How long does it take, and do we need planning permission?
Most sites go from survey to switch-on in 6 to 10 weeks, with 1 to 3 days on site. Most commercial rooftop systems are exempt from planning permission, and we handle planning and ESB Networks paperwork where it's needed.
We rent our building. Can we still go solar?
Yes, with your landlord's consent for the roof. We regularly set up agreements that work for both sides, and landlords often welcome the upgrade.
