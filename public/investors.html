For investors

Invest in contracted Irish solar

Aura funds, builds and runs solar on Irish business roofs, then sells the power to each business under a long-term PPA at a fixed price per kWh. Every site we switch on becomes a contracted income stream backed by a real, working asset.

  • Contracted income

    Long-term PPAs at a fixed rate, paid by the business that uses the power.

  • Real, monitored assets

    Tier-1 panels and inverters, insured and monitored, with maintenance carried by Aura for the full term.

  • Demand on site

    We focus on businesses that use most of their power in daylight, so most of what each roof produces is used where it's made.

  • Policy tailwind

    Ireland's Climate Action Plan targets 80% renewable electricity and 8 GW of solar by 2030.
