Privacy notice

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This notice explains what [Aura Energy Limited] ("Aura Energy", "we") does with personal information on auraenergy.ie. We keep it short, because we collect very little.

Who we are

[Aura Energy Limited], registered in Ireland, company no. [000000], registered office [street address], Dublin. We are the controller for the information described here. You can reach us at info@auraenergy.ie.

What we collect, and why

Enquiry forms

When you send the quote form, the roof estimate form or the investor form, we receive what you type: your name, work email, company, and the details of your site or enquiry, plus your phone number and Eircode if you choose to give them. We use this to respond, to prepare your free assessment or quote, and to follow up on it. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries about our services.

Enquiries are delivered to our team by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which processes the enquiry solely to deliver it, with your email address set as the reply-to.

Aura AI, the chat assistant

Aura AI is an AI assistant. What you type in the chat is sent through our server to Anthropic, the AI provider whose Claude model generates the replies. The conversation is kept only in your browser while you are on the page and is gone when you leave or start a new chat. We do not link chat messages to your identity, and we ask you not to type personal or contact details into the chat; the quote form is the place for those. Anthropic processes chat text as our service provider and states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default.

If you use the optional microphone button, your browser's own speech service turns your voice into text, which can involve the browser's maker (for example Google on Chrome) processing the audio. Only the transcribed text reaches us. The microphone is only active while you use it, and your browser asks for permission first.

Hosting and security

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the site, prevent abuse and rate-limit our forms and chat. We see short-lived technical logs, not browsing profiles.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. The forms, the estimator and the chat all work without storing anything on your device. If that ever changes, we will update this notice and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers named above, and only so the site can work. We do not sell or rent personal information. We would share information if the law required it, or with professional advisers under confidentiality.

Where it goes

Some of our providers process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as an EU adequacy decision or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to deal with your request and for up to [24 months] afterwards, unless you become a customer, in which case the details become part of the customer relationship. Technical logs are short-lived.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal information, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or ask for it in a portable form. Email info@auraenergy.ie and we will respond within a month. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.
