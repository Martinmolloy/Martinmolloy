Why go solar

What solar does for your home

Smaller bills

Every unit your roof makes is one you don't buy. A battery or diverter lets you use more of it yourself.

A price that doesn't rise

The power from your roof costs the same in year ten as it does on day one, whatever happens to energy prices.

A better BER

Solar lifts your home's Building Energy Rating, which buyers look at and green mortgages are based on.

Paid for what you export

Spare power flows to the grid, and your electricity supplier pays you for it under the Clean Export Guarantee.
