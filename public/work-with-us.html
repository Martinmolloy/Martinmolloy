Commercial Sales Manager

Full-timeDublin and nationwideSales View roleHide role

You'll win commercial solar customers for Aura. Irish businesses either buy the power from a system on their own roof through a Solar PPA, or buy the system outright, and you'll own each sale from the first conversation to the signed agreement.

What you'll do

  • Find and qualify businesses with suitable roofs and high daytime power use.
  • Run site visits and bill reviews, and present PPA and capex options side by side.
  • Build proposals with the team, negotiate terms and close agreements.
  • Keep the pipeline up to date and forecast honestly.
  • Stay the customer's main contact through survey and install.

What you'll bring

  • A track record in B2B sales, ideally in energy, solar, construction or another technical product.
  • Confidence explaining tariffs, kWh and payback to owners and finance directors.
  • A network across Irish businesses, or the drive to build one.
  • A full driving licence and willingness to travel to sites around Ireland.

Good to have

  • Experience selling PPAs or energy contracts.
  • Knowledge of SEAI grants and the Accelerated Capital Allowance.

Operations Intern

InternshipDublinOperations View roleHide role

You'll help run solar projects from signed agreement to switch-on, keeping surveys, paperwork, equipment and installers on track. It's a practical start in renewable energy.

What you'll do

  • Track each project's surveys, ESB Networks paperwork, equipment orders and install dates.
  • Prepare site packs and documents for surveys and installs.
  • Coordinate with installers, suppliers and customers by phone and email.
  • Check how live systems are performing from monitoring data.
  • Improve our templates and processes as you go.

What you'll bring

  • Studying or recently graduated in engineering, energy, business or a related field.
  • Organised and reliable, and comfortable with spreadsheets.
  • Clear on the phone and in writing.
  • A genuine interest in renewable energy.

Good to have

  • A full driving licence.
  • Any experience in solar, construction or project coordination.
